# ANTI-NATIONALS — Design Bible
Version 3.0

## Vision
Single-player, third-person political satire in a fictional India-inspired North Indian city. Dark, uncomfortable comedy mixes grounded consequences with spectacular absurdity. Characters and events use invented names and details, while social and political parallels may be recognisable.

## Premise
The player arrives as a university student. A viral photo, video or post accidentally embarrasses a privileged politician's relative. The incident connects the player to overlapping campus, media, police, business and political networks. The player develops their own motivation through play.

## World and factions
A dense city expanded through connected districts: university, markets and transport, media offices, government/political district, residences, businesses and VIP areas. Prioritise density and accessible interiors over empty scale. Factions include political groups, student organisations, media, police, private interests and fixers. Each has motives, contradictions and internal rivalries.

## Gameplay
Third-person movement; contextual interactions, dialogue choices, gestures and items; confrontations and chases; cars, scooters, motorcycles and auto-rickshaws; wanted levels with political consequences; money, influence, customisation, day/night routines, map/journal and manual/autosaves.

## Living world
Hybrid crowd simulation. Nearby NPCs get richer behaviours; distant activity is simplified. Important NPCs have personalities, schedules, relationships and memories. Events can emerge without player initiation. Witnesses react differently based on personality and what they saw. Outcomes can escalate, disperse or affect later events. Time is adjustable; important changes should persist.

## Story and progression
Overlapping storylines can be pursued in different orders, ignored or derailed. Failed objectives open alternate routes; major events may resolve without the player. Progression rewards influence, contacts, access and faction standing. Potential paths include outsider influence, insider, public figure and fixer. Several endings and variations are planned. No mandatory morality meter.

## Visual and audio
Stylised characters and environments, cinematic lighting and weather, close third-person framing, many interiors, grounded animation that becomes exaggerated in absurd moments, clear HUD, in-world phone/journal, multilingual dialogue and dramatic music that clashes with ridiculous events. Voiced major conversations are a long-term goal subject to production scope.

## First prototype
Greybox university block, third-person movement, small NPC crowd, independent argument, personality-influenced reactions, several player interventions, evolving outcomes, clock, journal and saving. 50+ NPCs and the large map remain long-term goals.

## Unresolved
City/district names, exact cast and factions, precise opening incident, final Android controls, voice-production scope, asset choices and first release scope.
